# ATLAS Events & Entertainment

ATLAS Events & Entertainment is the governed live-entertainment operating layer for promoters, producers, venues, artists, festivals and event operators.

## Canonical flow

Artist → Promoter → Venue → Production → Ticket → Fan → Payment → Settlement.

## Reuse before duplication

- Hospitality owns properties, spaces and physical access-provider boundaries.
- CRM owns people, companies, deals and consent-aware relationship records.
- Finance/Accounting owns the ledger, payables, receivables and postings.
- Commerce/POS owns catalog, merchandise, F&B and order boundaries.
- Creator Studio owns approved image, video, audio and campaign assets.
- HR/Payroll owns worker identity, time and pay boundaries.
- ATLAS Identity, tenant isolation, RBAC and audit remain universal.

## Fail-closed external boundaries

No ticket, payment, artist-booking, publishing or access-provider integration may be represented as live without authorized provider evidence. UI must expose truthful disconnected/authorization-required states.

## Product surfaces

Event pipeline; talent and venue coordination; contracts/riders; budgets and P&L; production/run-of-show; vendors; permits/compliance; staffing; equipment; ticketing/capacity/access; sponsors; merchandise/F&B; fan CRM; refunds/chargebacks; settlement; live command; post-event analytics.

## Network

ATLAS Network may expose marketplace discovery and governed handoff between artists, promoters, venues and vendors. Transactions remain organization-scoped and must not bypass canonical Commerce, Finance, Identity or audit controls.
